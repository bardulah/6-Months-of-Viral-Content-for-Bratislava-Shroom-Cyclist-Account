# Story Content Calendar

Daily story ideas to boost engagement and drive traffic to main posts. Stories = casual, raw, real-time.

---

## Story Strategy

**Post frequency:** 5-10 stories per day
**Purpose:** Stay top-of-mind, drive feed engagement, build intimacy
**Vibe:** Behind-the-scenes, unfiltered, conversational

---

## Story Series Ideas

### "Morning Minute"
Daily 60-second ramble while cycling. Raw, unedited, just thoughts.

### "What I'm Betting"
Pre-game breakdown of one pick. Show the logic, not just the pick.

### "Grow Diary"
Daily check-in during critical phases (colonization, pinning, fruiting).

### "Spot Tease"
3-story sequence: Clue → Hint → Reveal (drives to feed post)

### "AMA Fridays"
Q&A sticker → answer 10-15 throughout the day

### "Walk and Talk"
Post-ride reflection. Phone in hand, walking, decompressing.

## Story Best Practices

### Technical
- Vertical format only (1080x1920)
- Add captions to all video
- Use native features (polls, stickers, etc.)
- Keep text readable (large, high contrast)

### Content
- First story should hook (no slow builds)
- Don't over-produce—raw wins
- Show face sometimes (builds trust)
- Mix photo/video/text-only

### Timing
- Space throughout day (not all at once)
- Post when doing the thing (real-time)
- Story before feed post = prime the audience

### Engagement
- Always include interactive element
- Reply to every story response
- Use "seen by" list to identify super fans
- Reshare quality responses

## Feed Post Promotion

When new feed post goes live:

**Story 1 (immediately)**
- Share post to story
- Add "New post" sticker
- Brief teaser text

**Story 2 (2 hours later)**
- Highlight key quote/stat from post
- "Link in feed" text

**Story 3 (4-6 hours later)**
- Share best comment from post
- "The conversation is happening ⬆️"
