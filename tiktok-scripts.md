## Script 1: "The 6AM Question"
**Duration:** 45-60 seconds
**Best for:** Week 1, establishing persona

### Visuals
- POV cycling along Danube at sunrise
- Golden hour lighting
- Empty paths

### Script
```
[0:00 - 0:03]
*Sound of cycling, no music yet*

[0:03 - 0:08]
"Everyone asks me why I cycle at 6am."

[0:08 - 0:12]
*Beat*
"Wrong question."

[0:12 - 0:22]
"The question is: why are you NOT cycling at 6am?"

[0:22 - 0:30]
"While you're hitting snooze for the third time..."
*Cut to beautiful sunrise shot*
"...I'm watching the Danube wake up."

[0:30 - 0:40]
"While you're stuck in traffic at 8..."
*Speed up footage slightly*
"...I've already done 30 kilometers."

[0:40 - 0:50]
"They say early bird gets the worm. Nah."
*Slow down to contemplative pace*
"Early bird gets the whole city to themselves."

[0:50 - 0:58]
*Pause, just cycling sounds*
"6am. Bratislava. This is living."

[0:58 - END]
*Hold on beautiful vista*
```

### Delivery Notes
- Calm, not preachy
- Let the visuals do work
- Pause after "wrong question" - let it land

"At 90... your mind joins them."
"It starts making excuses. 'This is far enough.' 'Nobody will know.'"

[0:45 - 0:58]
"But here's the thing..."
*Pause*
"At 100... you understand something."

[0:58 - 1:08]

*Let that land*

## Production Notes

### Audio
- Wind noise is okay - sounds authentic
- Don't over-produce
- Natural breathing during hard efforts = good
- Add subtle music in edit, not during recording

### Camera
- GoPro chest or helmet mount for POV
- Stabilization on
- 1080p minimum, 4K if possible
- Golden hour is your friend

### Editing
- Cut on beat if using music
- Text overlays for key phrases
- Don't over-edit - raw is better
- 5% rule: Only 5% of footage makes final cut

### Posting
- Caption should complement, not repeat script
- First comment with hashtags
- Pin a question in comments to drive engagement

---

*Film more than you need. Say less than you think. Let the journey speak.*
