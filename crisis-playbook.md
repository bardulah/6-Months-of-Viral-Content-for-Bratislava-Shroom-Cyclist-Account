# Crisis Playbook

What to do when things blow up—good or bad. Prepared responses for predictable situations.

---

## Core Principles

1. **Speed matters** - First 2-4 hours shape the narrative
2. **Never be defensive** - Agree and amplify OR ignore
3. **Stay in character** - Don't break persona under pressure
4. **Document everything** - Screenshots before blocking
5. **This too shall pass** - Internet moves fast

---

## Scenario 1: Going Viral (Good Crisis)

### Signs
- Notifications exploding
- New follower surge
- Post passing 100x normal engagement
- DMs overwhelming

### Immediate Actions (First 2 Hours)

**Hour 1:**
- Reply to EVERY comment possible (seeds conversation)
- Pin a follow-up comment directing narrative
- Post 1-2 stories acknowledging the moment
- Don't sleep—ride the wave

**Hour 2-4:**
- Continue engaging
- Post follow-up content (new followers need more)
- Thank your community in stories
- Prepare for hate (always comes with virality)

### Content Strategy During Viral

**Post immediately:**
- Story: "Well this is happening"
- Pin comment: "For context..." or "More on this: [link to related content]"

**Post within 24 hours:**
- Follow-up feed post that capitalizes
- "Since [viral post] resonated, here's more on..."
- Give new followers a reason to stay

**Post within 48 hours:**
- Return to regular content
- Don't let one viral post define you
- Show depth beyond the hit

### What NOT to Do

- Don't brag or be cringe about it
- Don't immediately ask for follows
- Don't sell anything
- Don't abandon your audience for bigger accounts
- Don't change your content to chase the viral format

### Post-Viral Analysis

After 72 hours:
- What specifically went viral (hook? message? timing?)
- Where did views come from?
- What percentage followed?
- Can this be replicated authentically?

## Scenario 2: Hate Wave

### Signs
- Negative comments spiking
- Quote tweets dunking on you
- DMs turning hostile
- Followers dropping while engagement rises

### Immediate Response Framework

**Assess the source:**
1. Is this coordinated (one community/account)?
2. Is this organic backlash (your content triggered)?
3. Is this misunderstanding (you were unclear)?

**Response by type:**

**If coordinated attack:**
- Don't engage
- Mute/block aggressors
- Document everything
- Wait it out (usually 24-48 hours)
- Your community will defend you

**If organic backlash:**
- Consider if criticism is valid
- If valid: Acknowledge and learn
- If not valid: Ignore or one-time clarify

**If misunderstanding:**
- Clarify ONCE in comment or story
- Don't over-explain
- Then disengage

### Comment Response Strategy

**Hateful personal attacks:**
- Delete and block
- Screenshot first
- No engagement

**Criticism with some validity:**
- "Fair point. Here's my thinking: [brief]"
- One response, then disengage

**Bad faith arguments:**
- "You might be right" (ends thread)
- Or complete ignore

**Accusations/lies:**
- One clarification with evidence
- Pin if necessary
- Then ignore

### What NOT to Do

- Don't go on a blocking spree publicly
- Don't post about "haters" repeatedly
- Don't engage in back-and-forth
- Don't private your account
- Don't apologize unless actually wrong

### Recovery

After hate wave passes:
- Post normal content as if nothing happened
- Thank community in low-key way
- Review what triggered it
- Decide if adjustment needed or stay course

## Scenario 3: Accusations & Misinformation

### Types

**"You're faking your bets"**
- Keep betting slip proof in saved stories
- Link to verification when asked
- Don't over-prove (looks desperate)

**"This is illegal"**
- Don't engage in legal debate
- Post disclaimer if appropriate
- Continue unless actually illegal

**"You're promoting harmful activity"**
- Depends on accusation
- Address if legitimate concern
- Ignore if bad faith

### Response Template

```
For clarity on [accusation]:

[One sentence factual response]

[Link to evidence if applicable]

Moving on now. More content coming.
```

### When to Actually Respond

**Respond if:**
- Misinformation is spreading widely
- Your community is asking
- It's affecting real opportunities

**Don't respond if:**
- It's from one angry person
- Responding will amplify
- Truth is already clear to your audience

## Scenario 4: Platform Issues

### Account Restricted/Suspended

**Immediate:**
- Screenshot everything
- Document what you posted recently
- File appeal immediately
- Don't panic publicly on other platforms

**If suspended:**
- Appeal using their process
- Be patient (can take weeks)
- If you have other platforms, light update only
- Don't create new account (permanent ban risk)

### Shadowban

**Signs:**
- Reach drops 80%+
- Hashtags stop working
- Content doesn't appear in explore

**Actions:**
- Don't post for 24-48 hours
- Remove recently used hashtags
- Avoid engagement baiting
- Return to normal posting slowly

### Content Removed

**If legitimately removed:**
- Learn from it
- Don't repost same content
- Appeal if you disagree

**If removed in error:**
- Appeal immediately
- Screenshot the content
- Document removal reason

## Scenario 5: Doxxing or Serious Threats

### Immediate Actions

1. **Screenshot everything** before it disappears
2. **Don't engage** with the threat publicly
3. **Report to platform** (use their serious threat forms)
4. **Document timeline** of events
5. **Increase privacy** - review what's public

### If Personal Info Leaked

- Don't confirm or deny
- Don't address publicly
- Increase security (change passwords, 2FA)
- Consider police report if threats are serious

### When to Contact Authorities

- Specific threats of violence
- Stalking behavior
- Leaked home address
- Threats against family

## Scenario 6: Mental Health Crisis (Yours)

### Signs

- Checking notifications obsessively
- Mood dependent on engagement
- Dreading posting
- Can't disconnect
- Comparison spiral

### Immediate Actions

- Take 24-48 hours off
- Post "Taking a brief break" if needed
- Don't check notifications
- Talk to real humans

### Structural Changes

- Set screen time limits
- Scheduled notification checks (not constant)
- Remember: This is a project, not your identity
- The account can survive a break

---

## Scenario 7: Legal/Official Contact

### If Contacted by Platform

- Read carefully
- Respond professionally
- Don't over-share
- Follow their process

### If Contacted by Media

- Don't respond immediately
- Verify they are who they say
- Decide if you want exposure
- If yes: Stay in character
- If no: Polite decline

### If Contacted by Authorities

- Don't panic
- Don't ignore
- Consult lawyer before responding
- Know your rights

### If Contacted by Lawyers (C&D etc.)

- Take seriously
- Don't respond immediately
- Consult your own lawyer
- Often just intimidation (but not always)

## Crisis Communication Templates

### General Clarification
```
Since there's confusion:

[Fact 1]
[Fact 2]

Back to regular content now. Thanks for understanding.
```

### Acknowledging Mistake
```
I got this wrong.

[What happened]
[What I should have done]
[What I'll do differently]

Appreciate those who called it out.
```

### Addressing False Accusation
```
For the record:

[Truth with evidence]

Not engaging further on this. Content continues.
```

### Taking a Break
```
Taking a few days offline.

[Brief reason if you want]

See you [timeframe].
```

### Coming Back After Break
```
Back.

[Optional: very brief what you learned]

Let's continue.
```

## Preventive Measures

### Regular Backups
- Screenshot best content
- Save your captions externally
- Export follower data (where possible)
- Don't have single point of failure

### Privacy Hygiene
- Different email for socials
- No personal info in posts
- Scrub metadata from photos
- VPN for location privacy

### Community Safety Net
- Build relationships with other creators
- They can verify if you're shadowbanned
- They can amplify if you need support
- Don't be isolated

### Mental Guardrails
- Scheduled posting where possible
- Notification batching
- One day per week truly offline
- Remember why you started

---

## Post-Crisis Review

After any crisis, ask:

1. What triggered it?
2. How did I respond?
3. What worked?
4. What would I do differently?
5. What structural changes prevent this?

---

## The Meta-Rule

**Most "crises" aren't crises.**

They feel huge in the moment. In 48 hours, the internet has moved on.

Your job:
1. Don't make it worse
2. Protect what matters (safety, reputation, community)
3. Keep creating

The account survives almost everything. Your peace of mind matters more.

---

*Prepare for crisis before crisis. When you're in it, you won't have time to think.*
