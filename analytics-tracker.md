# Analytics Tracker Template

What to measure, how to measure it, and when to pivot. Data-driven content optimization.

---

## Core Metrics Framework

### Vanity vs. Actionable Metrics

**Vanity (feels good, less useful):**
- Total followers
- Total likes
- Total views

**Actionable (drives decisions):**
- Engagement rate
- Save rate
- Share rate
- Follower growth rate
- Profile visits per post

**Focus on actionable metrics. They tell you what to do next.**

## Benchmark Targets

### Follower Growth
- **Healthy:** 5-15% monthly growth
- **Strong:** 15-30% monthly growth
- **Viral:** 30%+ monthly growth

### Engagement Rate
- **Low:** <1%
- **Average:** 1-3%
- **Good:** 3-6%
- **Excellent:** 6%+

Note: ER typically drops as followers grow. A 10k account with 5% ER is excellent.

### Save Rate (Key Metric)
- **Low:** <1% of likes
- **Good:** 3-5% of likes
- **Excellent:** 5%+ of likes

Saves = algorithm signal that content is valuable

### Share Rate
- **Average:** 1-2% of likes
- **Good:** 3-5% of likes
- **Viral potential:** 5%+ of likes

Shares = best growth mechanism

## Red Flags & Pivot Triggers

### When to Pivot Content Type

**Trigger:** Content pillar consistently underperforms for 3+ weeks
**Action:** Reduce frequency, test variations, or retire

**Trigger:** New content type outperforms everything
**Action:** Increase frequency, explore variations

### When to Pivot Posting Time

**Trigger:** Consistent low engagement at certain times for 2+ weeks
**Action:** Move that content type to different slot

### When to Pivot Hook Style

**Trigger:** First 1-second drop-off increasing
**Action:** Test new hook styles, analyze what's working

### When to Pivot Platform Focus

**Trigger:** One platform significantly outperforming for 4+ weeks
**Action:** Shift more energy there, maintain baseline on others

## Content Experiments Template

### Hypothesis
```
If I [change], then [expected result] because [reasoning].
```

### Test Setup
```
Experiment: ___________
Control: [normal version]
Variable: [what you're changing]
Sample size: [X posts]
Duration: [X weeks]
Success metric: ___________
```

### Results
```
Control performance: ___________
Variable performance: ___________
Difference: ___________%
Conclusion: ___________
Action: ___________
```

### Experiments to Run

**Timing experiments:**
- Same content, different days
- Same content, different times
- Week A pattern vs Week B pattern

**Hook experiments:**
- Question vs statement
- Controversial vs agreeable
- Personal vs universal

**Format experiments:**
- Carousel vs single image
- Video length variations
- Text overlay vs caption only

**Content mix experiments:**
- More memes vs more philosophy
- More personal vs more educational
- More talking head vs more POV

## Quick Reference: What Metric Tells You What

| Metric | What It Indicates | Action If Low |
|--------|-------------------|---------------|
| Reach | Content distribution | Better hooks, hashtags |
| Likes | Instant reaction | Better content quality |
| Comments | Engagement depth | More questions, CTAs |
| Saves | Long-term value | More educational/useful |
| Shares | Viral potential | More relatable/universal |
| Follows | Profile appeal | Better bio, consistent content |
| Watch time | Content quality (video) | Tighter editing, better hooks |
| Story replies | Community intimacy | More interactive elements |

## The 80/20 Rule of Analytics

**Focus 80% of attention on:**
- Engagement rate
- Save rate
- Share rate
- Follower growth rate
- Top/bottom posts comparison

**Check but don't obsess over:**
- Total likes
- Total followers
- Impressions/reach
- Follower count

---

*Measure what matters. Ignore what doesn't. And remember: Analytics inform decisions—they don't make them. You still need creative instinct.*
