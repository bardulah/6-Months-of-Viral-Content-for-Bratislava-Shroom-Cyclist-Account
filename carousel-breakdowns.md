# Instagram Carousel Breakdowns

10-slide carousels for maximum saves and shares. These expand key philosophy posts into swipeable content.

---

## Design Guidelines

**Font:** Clean sans-serif (Inter, Helvetica, SF Pro)
**Background:** Solid colors or subtle gradients
**Text:** High contrast, centered
**Slides 1 & 10:** Hook and CTA
**Slides 2-9:** One idea per slide

---

## Carousel Design Notes

### Color Schemes

**Scheme 1: Dark Wisdom**
- Background: #1a1a1a
- Text: #ffffff
- Accent: #d4af37 (gold)

**Scheme 2: Clean Minimal**
- Background: #fafafa
- Text: #1a1a1a
- Accent: #4a90d9 (blue)

**Scheme 3: Nature**
- Background: #2d3436
- Text: #f5f5f5
- Accent: #00b894 (green)

### Typography Hierarchy
- Main text: Bold, 48-72pt
- Subtext: Regular, 24-36pt
- CTA: Medium, 32pt

### Slide Timing
- Slides 1-2: Hook (fast swipe expected)
- Slides 3-8: Content (slower, digesting)
- Slide 9: Build to conclusion
- Slide 10: CTA (linger here)

- **Tuesday, Thursday, Sunday** = Best carousel days
- **Post time:** 12:00-14:00 or 19:00-21:00 Bratislava
- **Stories:** Share each slide separately throughout day
- **First comment:** Hashtags + question
- **Pin comment:** Highlighting key insight
