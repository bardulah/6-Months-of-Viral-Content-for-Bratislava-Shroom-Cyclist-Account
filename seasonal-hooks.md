## Internet & Cultural Moments

### Trending Hashtag Tie-Ins

**When something trends:**
1. Does it relate to your pillars?
2. Can you add unique perspective?
3. Don't force it—only if authentic

**Examples:**
- #MentalHealthAwareness → Cycling as therapy
- #QuitYourJob → Your story
- #PlantBased → Mushroom cultivation
- #FridayFeeling → "My Friday doesn't need a mood"

### Meme Formats

**When new format trends:**
- Quick execution (hours, not days)
- Apply to your pillars
- Don't overdo (one use maximum)

### Audio Trends

**Seasonal sounds:**
- Cozy autumn sounds in fall
- Christmas sounds ironically
- New year motivation sounds (subvert them)

## Content Calendar Integration

### Monthly Planning

At month start:
1. List all events/holidays this month
2. Identify which align with pillars
3. Pre-plan 3-4 seasonal posts
4. Leave room for spontaneous tie-ins

### Weekly Check

- What's happening this week culturally?
- Any sporting events to leverage?
- Any trending moments to join?

### Daily Awareness

- Check trending topics
- Look for quick tie-in opportunities
- Don't force—only join if authentic

## Seasonal Content Examples

### Example: New Year's Post (January 1)

**Hook:** "New year, same system"

**Caption:**
```
Everyone's setting resolutions.
New gym memberships. New diets. New "this year will be different."

Here's my resolution: Keep doing exactly what I've been doing.

Because when you've already designed a life you love, the goal isn't change.

It's continuation.

Same bike. Same mushrooms. Same bets. Same freedom.

Year 2 of not waiting for permission.

Happy new year. Don't change. Evolve.
```

**Image prompt:** Bike on same route as Day 1, year later, comparison energy

---

### Example: Tour de France Post (July Mountain Stage)

**Hook:** "Watch them suffer. Then go suffer yourself."

**Caption:**
```
Tour Stage 17: Col du Galibier

Watching them climb 2,642m while I drink coffee.

Then I go do my local 500m climb.

Not the same. Not even close.

But the lesson is the same: The suffering is the point.

They suffer to win races.
I suffer to understand myself.

Different mountains. Same teacher.

Going to ride now. Altitude: 450m. Same energy.
```

**Image prompt:** Split screen: Tour climbing and your local climb

---

### Example: November 17 Post (Slovak Freedom Day)

**Hook:** "Freedom isn't given. It's practiced."

**Caption:**
```
November 17, 1989: Students marched for freedom.

November 17, 2024: I'm practicing it.

Freedom isn't a destination. It's a daily practice.

Every morning I choose my schedule: practice.
Every ride with no destination: practice.
Every day without asking permission: practice.

The revolution gave us the right.
It's on us to use it.

Most people won the freedom lottery and still live like prisoners.

Wake up.

—

17. november 1989: Študenti pochodovali za slobodu.
17. november 2024: Ja ju praktizujem.
```

**Image prompt:** Historical image reference with modern cycling parallel

## Quick Reference: When to Post What

| Event Type | When to Post | Post Type |
|------------|--------------|-----------|
| Major holiday | Day of | Philosophy + photo |
| Sporting event | Before, during, after | Preview, updates, analysis |
| Weather milestone | Day it happens | Cycling POV |
| Trending moment | Within hours | Quick meme/take |
| Season change | First relevant day | Transition content |
| Tournament | Throughout | Daily light + key moments heavy |

---

*Seasonal content isn't about being topical. It's about showing your lens on what everyone else is seeing. Same event, different perspective. That's the value.*
