## Hook Formulas

### The Pattern Interrupt
`[Common belief] + [Contradiction]`
- "Your comfort zone isn't comfortable. It's just familiar."

### The Open Loop
`[Intriguing setup] + [Promise of revelation]`
- "There's a place in Bratislava nobody talks about. Here's why."

### The Flex Disguised as Lesson
`[Impressive stat] + [Educational frame]`
- "€20 → €847. Here's the thesis."

### The Relatability Trap
`[Universal experience] + [Unexpected twist]`
- "Everyone asks why I cycle at 6am. Wrong question."

### The Status Challenge
`[What 'they' do] + [What you do differently]`
- "You pay €80/hour for therapy. I pay €0 to dissolve my problems."

## A/B Testing Hooks

For each major post, prepare 2-3 hook variants:

**Example - Century Ride Post:**
- A: "100km. First time. Here's what happened at kilometer 80."
- B: "The impossible became routine. Today's ride:"
- C: "My legs wanted to quit at 80km. My mind at 90km. At 100km I understood."

Test different hooks on different platforms or times. Track which style gets best engagement for your audience.

## Platform-Specific Optimization

### TikTok
- First 1 second is everything
- Use text overlay with hook
- Don't start with "Hey guys" or "So..."
- Pattern interrupt immediately

### Instagram Reels
- Same as TikTok
- Can be slightly longer hook (2-3 sec)
- Hashtags in caption, not video

### Twitter
- First line must be complete thought
- No "Thread:" - just start
- Controversial > Agreeable for RT

### Instagram Feed
- First line before "...more"
- 125 characters visible
- End first line with intrigue
