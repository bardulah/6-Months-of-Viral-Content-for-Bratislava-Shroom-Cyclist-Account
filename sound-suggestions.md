# Sound & Music Suggestions

Audio strategy for TikTok and Reels. Trending sounds change fast—this is a framework plus evergreen options.

---

## Sound Strategy

**Rule 1:** Trending audio = algorithmic boost
**Rule 2:** Match audio energy to content energy
**Rule 3:** Original audio builds brand (voiceovers)
**Rule 4:** Some content needs NO music (authenticity)

---

## Content-to-Audio Matching

### Cycling POV Content

**High energy (downhill, fast rides):**
- Electronic/EDM with build-ups
- High BPM (120+)
- Drop timing with speed moments

**Chill rides (sunrise, contemplative):**
- Lo-fi beats
- Ambient electronic
- Acoustic instrumental

**Epic achievement (century rides, milestones):**
- Cinematic builds
- Orchestral swells
- "Main character" energy tracks

**Raw/authentic:**
- No music—just wind, breathing, bike sounds
- Nature sounds enhanced

---

### Philosophy Content

**Thought-provoking:**
- Minimal piano
- Ambient pads
- Silence with subtle texture

**Confrontational takes:**
- Darker electronic
- Bass-heavy minimal
- Tension builds

**Uplifting realizations:**
- Hopeful acoustic
- Gentle builds
- Resolution sounds

---

### Betting Content

**Wins:**
- Celebration sounds
- Cash register dings
- Hype tracks (careful—can be cringe)

**Analysis/educational:**
- No music or very subtle
- Focus on voice
- Professional podcast vibe

**Losses/lessons:**
- Melancholic but not sad
- Reflective tones
- "Dust yourself off" energy

---

### Hidden Spot Reveals

**Mystery build:**
- Suspenseful ambient
- Building anticipation
- Reveal moment sync

**Cozy spots (bars, food):**
- Warm acoustic
- Jazz undertones
- Inviting atmosphere

**Nature spots:**
- Minimal or no music
- Natural ambiance enhanced
- Birds, water, wind

---

### Grow Updates

**Timelapse:**
- Building electronic
- Progressive house
- Growth = tempo increase

**Chill updates:**
- Lo-fi beats
- Study music
- Patient vibes

**Harvest celebration:**
- Triumphant but subtle
- Achievement unlocked
- Not over-the-top

## Platform-Specific Notes

### TikTok
- Trending audio = major algorithm boost
- Use "Add Sound" page to find what's hot
- Save sounds when you hear them on FYP
- Original sounds can trend if content hits

### Instagram Reels
- Shares many trends with TikTok (delayed)
- "Use Audio" button shows how many used it
- Instagram music library is more limited
- Original audio still works well

### Both Platforms
- First 1-3 seconds: audio should hook or complement hook
- Audio peaks should sync with visual peaks
- Don't let music overpower voiceover

## No Music Content

Sometimes silence (or natural sound) is better:

**Use no music when:**
- Raw authenticity is the point
- The message is heavy/serious
- Natural sounds tell the story
- Voiceover is the main content

**Natural audio to enhance:**
- Bike chain sounds
- Heavy breathing on climbs
- Wind on descents
- City ambiance
- Bird songs on trails
- Rain on helmet

## How to Find Trending Sounds

### TikTok
1. Scroll FYP, save sounds you hear repeatedly
2. Check "Discover" → "Sounds"
3. Look at what sounds competitors use
4. Search keywords + filter by "sounds"

### Instagram
1. Check Reels tab for repeating audio
2. "Save Audio" when you hear trending
3. Look at audio use count (higher = trending)
4. Follow audio trend accounts

### Tools
- TokBoard (trending sounds tracker)
- TrendTok analytics
- Social media news accounts

## Licensing Considerations

### Safe to Use
- TikTok/Instagram music library
- Royalty-free (Epidemic Sound, Artlist)
- Creative Commons licensed
- Original audio you create

### Risky
- Mainstream copyrighted music (varies by platform)
- Uncleared samples
- Anything you're not sure about

### Business Account Note
- Business accounts have limited music libraries
- Creator accounts = full library access
- Stay creator account for full audio access

## Audio Editing Tips

### Timing
- Cut to beat (not randomly)
- Drop = reveal or climax
- Build = anticipation
- Silence = emphasis

### Volume
- Voiceover louder than music
- Duck music during speech
- Fade out, don't hard cut

### Sync Points
- Visual cuts on beats
- Speed changes on drops
- Text appears on hits

## Content-Specific Recommendations

### "The 6AM Question" script
- Start with natural sounds only
- Subtle lo-fi enters at "wrong question"
- Builds through the content
- Fade out at end

### Century Ride Milestone
- Cinematic build throughout
- Peak at completion moment
- Emotional resolution

### Philosophy One-Liner
- Minimal—maybe just ambient pad
- Or complete silence
- Let words do the work

### Hidden Spot Reveal
- Mystery build → reveal drop
- Match energy of the spot
- Cozy = warm, adventure = epic

### Betting Slip Posts
- Keep it subtle or silent
- Focus should be on information
- Light background texture maximum

## Weekly Audio Task

**Every week:**
1. Spend 10 min on FYP saving new sounds
2. Note which sounds show up 3+ times
3. Match saved sounds to upcoming content
4. Test 1-2 new trending sounds per week

---

*Audio is 50% of video. Never an afterthought. But also: don't let trendy audio make you inauthentic. Sometimes wind on the mic is the perfect sound.*
