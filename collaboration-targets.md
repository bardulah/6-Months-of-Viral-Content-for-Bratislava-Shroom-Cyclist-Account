# Collaboration Targets

Strategic accounts to engage with, duet ideas, and cross-promotion opportunities. Organized by account type and audience overlap.

---

## Collaboration Philosophy

**Goal:** Access new audiences that overlap with yours
**Method:** Provide value before asking for anything
**Timeline:** 2-4 weeks of engagement before collab ask

---

## Target Account Categories

### Category 1: Cycling Accounts (High Overlap)

**Account Types to Target:**
- Urban cycling advocates
- Bikepacking/adventure cycling
- Commuter cycling content
- Cycling photography
- Bike mechanics/DIY

**Size Sweet Spot:** 5k-50k followers
**Why:** Same core activity, different angle

**Engagement Strategy:**
1. Comment genuinely on their rides for 2 weeks
2. Share their content to your stories
3. DM with specific compliment + collab idea
4. Offer: Joint ride, route share, gear review

**Collab Ideas:**
- Trade routes (you share BA, they share their city)
- Joint sunrise ride (if in same city)
- "Day in the life" takeovers
- Gear comparison content
- "Why we ride" parallel posts

**Duet/Stitch Ideas:**
- Stitch their hot take with your counter
- Duet their beautiful shot with your local version
- React to their milestone with your journey

## Specific Collaboration Formats

### The Takeover
- You post on their account for 24 hours
- Show your day through their lens
- They do same on yours
- Announce beforehand for hype

### The Challenge
- Create joint challenge with hashtag
- Both participate and engage entries
- Builds both communities simultaneously
- Example: "One week no alarm challenge"

### The Swap
- Trade content (route for route, spot for spot)
- Gives each other's audience value
- Easy to execute, clear value exchange

### The Interview
- IG Live or recorded video
- Ask each other questions
- Intimate, builds trust
- Goes on both feeds

### The Reaction Chain
- You react to them
- They react to your reaction
- Goes back and forth
- Built-in content series

### The Event
- Joint ride/activity
- Document together
- Each posts their version
- Same event, different perspectives

## DM Templates for Collab Asks

### Initial Approach (After 2+ Weeks Engaging)

**Template 1 - Value First:**
```
Hey [name],

Been following your [specific content type] for a while - your [specific post] really nailed [specific thing].

I make similar content around [your angle] and think our audiences would love a crossover.

Idea: [specific collab concept]

No pressure either way - just thought it could be fun for both sides. Let me know if you're down.

[Your name]
```

**Template 2 - Specific Ask:**
```
[Name],

Quick idea: What if we did [specific collab]?

I've got [X] followers in [demographic], you've got [Y] in [demographic]. Overlap seems strong.

I'd do [your contribution]. You'd do [their contribution].

Thoughts?
```

**Template 3 - Mutual Benefit:**
```
Hey,

Noticed we both [shared interest/approach].

Want to trade [specific thing]? I'll share [your offer] with my audience, you share [your ask] with yours.

Win-win. LMK.
```

### Follow-Up (If No Response in 1 Week)

```
Hey - just bumping this in case it got buried. No worries if the timing's not right. Always down to collab in future if it makes sense.
```

### After Successful Collab

```
That went really well - [specific positive result].

Appreciate you being open to it. Let's do round 2 sometime. I'm thinking [next idea] when the timing's right.

Keep crushing.
```

## Cross-Promotion Outside Collabs

### Shoutout Strategy
- Genuine, not transactional
- Only accounts you actually enjoy
- Mix sizes (don't just shout out bigger accounts)
- Tag properly so they see it

### Content Sharing
- Share their posts to your stories
- Add your own commentary/addition
- They often share back or engage
- Builds relationship before ask

### Comment Collaboration
- Engage consistently on same accounts
- They'll recognize you
- "Hey, I see you everywhere too"
- Natural relationship building

---

## Accounts to Avoid

**Red Flags:**
- All engagement seems purchased
- No genuine community interaction
- Constantly selling/promoting
- Content feels inauthentic
- Bad reputation in niche

**Why Avoid:**
- Association hurts your credibility
- Their audience won't convert
- Wasted time and effort
- Can damage your brand

## Timeline to 10k via Collabs

**Month 1-2:** Foundation
- Build relationship with 15-20 accounts
- Consistent genuine engagement
- No asks yet

**Month 3:** First Collabs
- Pitch 5 accounts
- Aim for 2-3 yeses
- Execute and measure

**Month 4-5:** Scale
- Pitch 8-10 accounts
- Execute 4-5 collabs
- Build on what works

**Month 6:** Authority
- Bigger accounts now approachable
- Inbound collab requests start
- Choose selectively
